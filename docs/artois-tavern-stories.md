# Tavern rumours and news

At Agincourt Planet Port, enter The Hollow Crown with **3**. Gold **!** markers retain faction contracts. **Blue speech markers** open ordinary locals’ conversations. Click the **notice board** or **innkeeper**, or press **6 / Artois Gazette**, for news. Select topics or articles in the left-hand list. Escape closes reading before the pause menu; I/F2 can open the character sheet and return to the same conversation. Reading blocks launch/market hotkeys and freezes the parallax camera while lighting and normal docked traffic continue.

The cast adds eight newly generated sprites in a transparent 4×2 atlas: Mabel Copperweight (copper factor), Osei Bellows (Embry miner), Agnes Harrow (grain grower), Martin Quay (dockworker), Walter Penn (smallholder), Awa Threadgold (wool merchant), Jehan Ash (veteran), and Elin Rivet (shipwright). Each landing chooses three, alongside 2–4 faction contacts and one innkeeper. Locals have separate seats and click areas; the pending/eligible mission contacts remain guaranteed. The empty scene still starts with no people.

Sixteen authored conversations cover coinage and copper, taxes, tolls, wages, ore, wool, repairs and unproven raider suspicions. Each speaker also offers a verified trade tip from actual market configuration: planet grain to Border Post, planet oxygen to Embry, or Embry argent ore to the planet. Margins use purchase price at origin and resale price at destination. Unsupported/unprofitable routes produce no tip. Talk does not change prices, inventory, credits or reputation.

The Gazette selects four of eight authored dispatches per landing: provisioning, coinage complaints, a petition to the lord, Embry approaches, faction patrols, raider hearsay, contracts and Hanseatic trade. Reports are labelled as notices, correspondence or unconfirmed reports. They are authored world flavour, not an event-driven economy or fabricated records of NPC battles. A separate captain’s docket reads current mission, credits, cargo and naval reputation when opened. News and people remain stable during one visit; a new landing rerolls them. No explanation for the system closure is introduced.

Content is in `data/artois_tavern_stories.json`; selection and quote-derived tips are in `artois_tavern_stories.gd`. `artois_tavern_panel.gd` owns the reusable scrollable reading UI. `artois_tavern_notice.gd` draws the diegetic notice board; actor art remains independently removable.

Existing tavern, faction-patron clicks, character/mission tiers, randomized-contract and mouse/menu suites also pass. Rendered previews are in `tavern-stories-previews/`.
